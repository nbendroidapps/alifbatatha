Privacy Policy (গোপনীয়তার নীতি)

Effective Date: May 21, 2024

আলিফ বা তা ছা বাংলা আরবি (referred to as "the App") is developed by Md Mamunur Rasid. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience while using our application.

1 Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

When you sign in using Google Auth, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Picture URL (for display purposes within the App)
  • User ID (UID)

This information is stored securely in Google Firebase and is used to manage your account, save your learning progress, and track your premium membership status.

B. Usage Data

We collect information about your progress through the 24 levels, exam scores, and streaks to provide a personalized learning experience.

C. Device Information

We may collect non-personal information such as:

  • Device model and OS version
  • Unique device identifiers (for AdMob advertisement purposes)
  • IP address

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide Services: To save your lesson progress and certificates.
  • Premium Membership: To verify and manage your subscription plans.
  • Communication: To send important updates regarding your account or the App.
  • Advertising: To show relevant ads to free-tier users through Google AdMob.

3 Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Google AdMob (Privacy Policy)

4 Payment Security

The App uses a manual bKash payment system for premium upgrades. We do not store your financial credentials (like PINs or OTPs). We only store the Transaction ID and Mobile Number provided by you for the purpose of manual verification of the payment.

5 Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for users of all ages, including children learning the Quran. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

6 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
